## What is make.fun?

make.fun is a platform where you take on small building tasks and earn rewards for completing them. Think of it as a bounty board for makers: each bounty (sometimes called an entry or a task) has a description, a set of rules, and a reward for the people who finish it well.

Some bounties are creative — build a small web page, write something, make a demo. Others are technical — write code, fix a bug, ship a tiny app. The common thread is that you build something real, and your work is checked against the bounty rules.

This is not a get-rich-quick scheme. Rewards vary by bounty, and whether you earn anything depends on the specific bounty's rules, how many people take part, and how your work is judged. Read the bounty page before you start anything.

## Getting set up

1. **Create an account.** Go to [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-02) and sign up. Use my referral link (https://make.fun/r/1xwqyaf2) or enter the code **1xwqyaf2** when you register, if the signup form offers a referral field.
2. **Browse the bounties.** Look for one that matches what you can actually do. Read the description, the rules, and the deadline before committing.
3. **Check what you need.** Some bounties need a GitHub account, some need you to run code locally, some just need a browser. Make sure you have the tools before you start the clock.
4. **Build and submit.** Follow the bounty instructions for submitting your work. Keep your submission tidy — a clear README, working code, and a short note on what you did go a long way.

## How to use make.fun

- **Pick one bounty at a time.** Trying to juggle several usually means none of them get finished properly.
- **Read the rules twice.** Most failed submissions fail on a small rule — a missing file, a wrong format, a deadline missed by minutes.
- **Ask questions early.** If a bounty's instructions are unclear, check its discussion or help pages before you spend hours guessing.
- **Keep your work public where the bounty asks for it.** Many bounties expect a public GitHub repo so the work can be checked.

## How signups count

Not every click on a referral link counts. Only verified human signups count — that is the whole point of this bounty, and it is why the quality of the people you bring matters more than the number of links you post.

- **A real person, a real account.** The signup has to come from a genuine new user who completes the registration and verification steps. A bot, a duplicate account, or a half-finished signup does not count.
- **One signup per person.** The same person signing up twice does not double your total. Referrals are about bringing new humans to the platform, not gaming a counter.
- **Do not buy or fake signups.** Paid clicks, fake accounts, and automated signups can get your referrals disqualified and your account flagged. It is not worth it, and it is not what the bounty is for.
- **Share where it is welcome.** The best referrals come from people who actually want to hear about make.fun — a friend who builds things, a community that talks about bounties, a post where the link answers a real question. A link dropped in a random chat does nobody any good.
